In Scrabble, each letter is assigned a point value based on its frequency of use in the English language. Here's a breakdown of the point value and frequency for each letter in Scrabble:

| Letter | Frequency (Number of Tiles) | Point Value |
|--------|------------------------------|-------------|
| A      | 9                            | 1           |
| B      | 2                            | 3           |
| C      | 2                            | 3           |
| D      | 4                            | 2           |
| E      | 12                           | 1           |
| F      | 2                            | 4           |
| G      | 3                            | 2           |
| H      | 2                            | 4           |
| I      | 9                            | 1           |
| J      | 1                            | 8           |
| K      | 1                            | 5           |
| L      | 4                            | 1           |
| M      | 2                            | 3           |
| N      | 6                            | 1           |
| O      | 8                            | 1           |
| P      | 2                            | 3           |
| Q      | 1                            | 10          |
| R      | 6                            | 1           |
| S      | 4                            | 1           |
| T      | 6                            | 1           |
| U      | 4                            | 1           |
| V      | 2                            | 4           |
| W      | 2                            | 4           |
| X      | 1                            | 8           |
| Y      | 2                            | 4           |
| Z      | 1                            | 10          |
| Blank  | 2 (wildcards, no points)     | 0           |

### Key Points:
- Common letters like **E**, **A**, **I**, **O**, **N**, **R**, **T**, **L**, and **S** have lower point values but appear more frequently.
- Rare letters like **Q**, **Z**, **J**, and **X** are worth more points but are much less frequent.
- Blanks (wildcards) can be used as any letter but have no point value.
